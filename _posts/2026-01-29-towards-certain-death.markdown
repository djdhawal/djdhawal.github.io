Hi everyone.

As time has passed, I have become more and more convinced that impossible is just a matter of perception. It is impossible for me to become the president of the United States. Not true. It is impossible for me to propose a thesis topic on message passing in graph neural networks without fully understanding either message passing or graph neural networks. Not true.

I am betting on the future. Nothing is impossible. This quarter I am learning about graphs, all sorts of graphs, and I'm crawling through the endless internet at my own merry pace, scraping all the content on graphs: how to cut them, juice them and rinse them dry. I think I have devised a very strange way of learning. It is not helpful at all, and it makes me feel smart but sound like an idiot all the time.

I end up doing a very detailed surface-level skim of a wide variety of literature on a topic, and then I convince myself that I understand it to some extent. I don't. I just know what some of the common abbreviations stand for, nothing more. Like, GNN stands for Generative Neural Network. Sorry, Graph. Graph Neural Network. What's nice is that someday a graph neural network might actually come and read this.

A graph neural network trying to understand another graph neural network.
